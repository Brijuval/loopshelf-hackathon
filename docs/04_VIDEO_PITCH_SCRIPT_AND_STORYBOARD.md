# Video Pitch Script & Storyboard (3–5 Minutes)

**Target Length:** ~3:45 minutes.  
**Tone:** Confident, problem-focused, pacing matching the prototype clicks.

---

### 0:00 - 0:25 (The Hook)
**Visual:** Presenter facing camera, looking slightly exasperated. Graphic pops up showing a ₹300 HDMI cable.
**Audio:** "You have a major presentation tomorrow. You need an HDMI cable. You could buy one online for ₹300. But wait... 20 meters away, in the room right down the hall, another student already owns one. So why are we buying things we only need for a few hours?"

### 0:25 - 0:55 (The Problem & The Waste)
**Visual:** Cut to an animation or slides showing the flowchart: BUY → USE ONCE → STORE → FORGET. 
**Audio:** "Across university campuses, thousands of students own items that sit completely unused, while other students are spending money buying the exact same things. Calculators, lab coats, tripods, Arduinos. We don't have a shortage of products on campus. We have a shortage of *access* to those products."

### 0:55 - 1:40 (Introducing LoopShelf)
**Visual:** Screen recording of the LoopShelf Welcome Screen starts. The words "Don't buy it. Loop it." are prominently displayed. 
**Audio:** "Enter LoopShelf. LoopShelf turns a university campus into a shared, decentralized inventory. Unlike marketplaces designed for buying and selling permanently, LoopShelf is designed entirely around short-term, urgent need. It connects students who need something for a few hours with verified students nearby who have it."

### 1:40 - 2:20 (The Demo Walkthrough)
**Visual:** Screen recording moves to the Home Screen. The mouse clicks "Arduino Kit" under quick requests. It transitions to the Search Results, then to the Item Details screen showing owner Aditi.
**Audio:** "Let’s look at how this works. I need an Arduino for a weekend project. I type it into LoopShelf. Instantly, I see that Aditi, who is just 280 meters away at the library, has one available. LoopShelf shows me a 'Borrowability Score', confirming I'm saving ₹1,200 by borrowing instead of buying. I request it. Aditi approves."

### 2:20 - 2:55 (The Handoff & Trust)
**Visual:** Screen recording shows the Digital Borrow Pass with the QR code. Then transitions back to presenter.
**Audio:** "We meet at the campus library, and scan the LoopShelf QR Borrow Pass. The item is handed over securely, the return time is logged, and when I bring it back, we both receive a reliability score update. It's fast, zero-friction, and completely safe because the entire network is gated by verified university IDs."

### 2:55 - 3:25 (The AI Intelligence Layer & Differentiation)
**Visual:** Show Slide 7 (AI Layer) or a graphic of data points connecting on a campus map.
**Audio:** "LoopShelf isn't just another classifieds board. We are implementing an AI intelligence layer that predicts campus demand. For example, if finals are approaching, our system alerts students to list their scientific calculators. It spots dormant inventory and prompts owners to put it back into circulation."

### 3:25 - 3:55 (The Impact - SDG 12)
**Visual:** Show the LoopShelf Impact Dashboard (Screen 7 of prototype) highlighting "₹1,850 avoided purchases" and "4 items kept in circulation." Add the UN SDG 12 logo on screen.
**Audio:** "The impact is measurable. By shifting from ownership to access, LoopShelf directly supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 12. Every successful borrow is a new purchase avoided, cutting down plastic packaging, shipping emissions, and electronic waste."

### 3:55 - 4:15 (The Ending)
**Visual:** Presenter facing camera. Final fade to the LoopShelf logo.
**Audio:** "We don't need everyone to own everything. We need everyone to have access to what they need. We are LoopShelf. Don't buy it. Loop it. Thank you."
